# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include empathy, constructive feedback, accountability, and respect for differing viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hateful conduct, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a private GitHub issue addressed to the repository owner. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly.
